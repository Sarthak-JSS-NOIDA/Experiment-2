About me

Hey, I'm Sarthak. I'm a student and developer who spends a probably unreasonable amount of time taking things apart just to understand how they work.

I started out by learning the basics of programming, but eventually realized that I enjoy building complete things much more than simply solving isolated problems. Since then, I've experimented with web development, backend systems, databases, machine learning, blockchain, and more recently, AI and LLM applications.

Most of my projects come from something I wanted to understand or build myself. I've built a blockchain voting application, experimented with visual workflow automation through HttpFlow, worked on finance-related applications, and made smaller developer tools along the way.

I'm especially interested in the parts of software that sit underneath the interface — APIs, databases, application architecture, and the way different systems communicate with each other. At the same time, I enjoy making the frontend actually feel good to use.

I'm still figuring out exactly where I want to take all of this, so for now I'm learning by building, breaking things, fixing them, and starting the next project when I inevitably get another idea.
